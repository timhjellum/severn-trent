The Challenge

Severn Trent Services' internal communications and everyday tools lived across disconnected systems, with no shared design or navigation. Employees had no single place to check safety announcements, find company news, or get support for their work. I was responsible for the creative design, information architecture, content migration, and front-end development of a new SharePoint intranet built to bring it all together.

My Role

Designing and building the company's front door.
